# 5. Microcontroller Socket

![Overview](/docs/microcontroller-socket/microcontroller-socket-overview.png)

The Mill-Max female sockets let you remove the nice!nano later without desoldering. Install two 12-pin female headers (315-43-112-41-003000) on the **top side** of the PCB.

1. Insert both 12-pin female headers into the PCB.
2. Secure everything with a piece of Kapton or painter's tape across the top.
   ![Mill-Max sockets taped](/docs/microcontroller-socket/microcontroller-socket-taped.png)
3. Flip the PCB over.
4. Solder the two corner pins of each socket first to lock them in place.
   ![Mill-Max sockets soldered extremities](/docs/microcontroller-socket/microcontroller-socket-soldered-extremities.png)
5. Check that the sockets are flush and straight.
6. Solder the remaining pins.
   ![Mill-Max sockets soldered](/docs/microcontroller-socket/microcontroller-socket-soldered.png)
7. Remove the tape.
