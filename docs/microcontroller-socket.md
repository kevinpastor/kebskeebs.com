# 5. Microcontroller Socket

![Mill-Max sockets overview](/docs/microcontroller-socket/microcontroller-socket-overview.png)

The Mill-Max female sockets let you remove the nice!nano later without desoldering. Install two 12-pin female headers (315-43-112-41-003000) on the **top side** of the PCB.

1. Insert both 12-pin female headers into the nice!nano footprint on the top side of the PCB.
2. To keep them aligned while soldering, insert the 12-pin pin headers (350-10-112-00-006000) into the sockets, then secure everything with a piece of Kapton or painter's tape across the top. Do not use the nice!nano for alignment.
   ![Mill-Max sockets taped](/docs/microcontroller-socket/microcontroller-socket-taped.png)
3. Flip the PCB over.
4. Solder the two corner pins of each socket first to lock them in place.
   ![Mill-Max sockets soldered extremities](/docs/microcontroller-socket/microcontroller-socket-soldered-extremities.png)
5. Check that the sockets are flush and straight, then solder the remaining pins.
   ![Mill-Max sockets soldered](/docs/microcontroller-socket/microcontroller-socket-soldered.png)
6. Remove the pin headers and tape. Do not solder the nice!nano yet.

The sockets have a small bump on the underside, so it is normal for them to sit about 1 mm above the PCB surface.

> **Warning:** Keep your iron at 300–320 °C for this step. The plastic housing can deform if overheated.

> **Verify:** The sockets should be straight and not tilted. Check all 24 joints for clean, shiny joints and no bridges.
