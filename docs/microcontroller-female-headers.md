# 5. Microcontroller Female Headers

![Overview](/docs/microcontroller-socket/microcontroller-socket-overview.png)

> Installing female headers and pin headers allows you to remove the microcontroller later on without having to desolder anything.

The female headers go on the **top side** of the PCB.

1. Insert both female headers into the PCB.
2. Tape both female headers down.
   ![Mill-Max sockets taped](/docs/microcontroller-socket/microcontroller-socket-taped.png)
3. Flip the PCB over.
4. Solder the two outer pins of each female header.
   ![Mill-Max sockets soldered extremities](/docs/microcontroller-socket/microcontroller-socket-soldered-extremities.png)
5. Check that the female headers are flush and straight.
6. Solder the remaining pins.
   ![Mill-Max sockets soldered](/docs/microcontroller-socket/microcontroller-socket-soldered.png)
7. Remove the tape.
