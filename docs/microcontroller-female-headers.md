# 5. Microcontroller Female Headers

![Overview](/docs/microcontroller-female-headers/overview.png)

1. Insert both female headers into on the top side of the PCB.
2. Tape down both female headers.

    ![Female headers taped](/docs/microcontroller-female-headers/step-2.png)

3. Flip over the PCB.
4. Solder the two outer pins of each female header.

    ![Female headers edge pins soldered](/docs/microcontroller-female-headers/step-4.png)

5. Check that the female headers are flush and straight.

    ![Female headers sitting perpendicular to the PCB](/docs/microcontroller-female-headers/step-5.png)

6. Solder the remaining pins.

    ![Female headers soldered](/docs/microcontroller-female-headers/step-6.png)

7. Remove the tape.
