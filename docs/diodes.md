# 1. Diodes

[![Overview](/docs/diodes/overview_thumbnail.png)](/docs/diodes/overview.png)

**Diodes are polarized; orientation matters.** The line on the diode body must align with the line marking on the PCB silkscreen.

[![Diode oriented properly beside its pad](/docs/diodes/diode-orientation_thumbnail.png)](/docs/diodes/diode-orientation.png)

The following steps apply to **all 35 diodes**. Complete all steps for each diode one at a time, or perform each step across all diodes before moving to the next.

1. Apply flux to the pads for a diode on the back side of the PCB.
    - This helps solder flow cleanly.

    [![Flux applied to the pads for a diode](/docs/diodes/step-1_thumbnail.png)](/docs/diodes/step-1.png)

2. Pre-tin the bottom-right pad.
    - This pad will be used to hold the diode in place.

    [![Pre-tinned bottom-right pad](/docs/diodes/step-2_thumbnail.png)](/docs/diodes/step-2.png)

3. Position the diode.
    - The cathode stripe on the diode body must be on the same side as the silkscreen marking.

    [![Diode positioned with tweezers on pre-tinned pad](/docs/diodes/step-3_thumbnail.png)](/docs/diodes/step-3.png)

4. Reheat the pre-tinned pad while pushing the diode into position.

    [![Diode with one side soldered](/docs/diodes/step-4_thumbnail.png)](/docs/diodes/step-4.png)

5. Solder the top-left pad.
    - Rotate the board beforehand if it makes it easier.

    [![Diode soldered on both pads](/docs/diodes/step-5_thumbnail.png)](/docs/diodes/step-5.png)

6. Repeat the previous steps for all remaining diodes.

    [![All 35 diodes soldered — overview](/docs/diodes/step-6_thumbnail.png)](/docs/diodes/step-6.png)
