# 1. Diodes

![Overview](/docs/diodes/diodes-overview.png)

The 35 SMD diodes (1N4148W) go on the **back side** of the PCB.

**Diodes are polarized — orientation matters.** The line on the diode body must align with the line marking on the PCB silkscreen. Reversing a diode will cause keys not to register.

![Diode orientation beside the pads](/docs/diodes/diode-orientation.png)

The following steps apply to **all 35 diodes**. Complete all steps for each diode one at a time, or perform each step across all diodes before moving to the next.

1. Apply a small amount of flux to the bottom-right pad of each diode footprint. This helps solder flow cleanly.
    ![Flux applied to the bottom-right pad of each diode footprint](/docs/diodes/diode-pads-flux.png)
2. Pre-tin the bottom-right pad of every diode footprint by applying a small amount of solder. This pad will hold the diode in place.
    ![Pre-tinned bottom-right pad](/docs/diodes/diode-soldered-pad.png)
3. Hold a diode with tweezers. Reheat the pre-tinned pad with the iron, then slide the diode into position. The cathode stripe on the diode body must align with the silkscreen marking.
    ![Diode positioned with tweezers on pre-tinned pad](/docs/diodes/diode-positioned-with-tweezers.png)
    ![Diode with one side soldered](/docs/diodes/diode-soldered-front-side-side-angle-view.png)
4. Let the solder cool, then solder the second (left) pad. Rotate the board if it makes the second pad easier to reach.
    ![Diode soldered on both pads](/docs/diodes/diode-soldered-back-side.png)
5. Repeat for all remaining diodes.
    ![All 35 diodes soldered — overview](/docs/diodes/diodes-soldered-overview.png)
    ![Close-up of soldered diodes](/docs/diodes/diodes-soldered.png)
