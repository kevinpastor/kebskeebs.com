# 7. Battery Connector

[![Battery connector overview](/docs/battery-connector/overview_thumbnail.png)](/docs/battery-connector/overview.png)

1. Insert the connector on the top side of the PCB.

    [![Battery connector positioned](/docs/battery-connector/step-1_thumbnail.png)](/docs/battery-connector/step-1.png)

2. Tape down the connector.

    [![Battery connector taped](/docs/battery-connector/step-2_thumbnail.png)](/docs/battery-connector/step-2.png)

3. Flip over the PCB.
4. Solder both pins.

    [![Battery connector soldered](/docs/battery-connector/step-4_thumbnail.png)](/docs/battery-connector/step-4.png)

5. Remove the tape.

    [![Battery connector soldered overview](/docs/battery-connector/step-5_thumbnail.png)](/docs/battery-connector/step-5.png)
