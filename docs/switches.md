# 14. Switches

[![Overview](/docs/switches/overview_thumbnail.png)](/docs/switches/overview.png)

1. Verify that the pins of the switches are straight.
    * Bent pins will not seat properly into the hot-swap sockets and can damage them.

    [![Switch underside](/docs/switches/step-1_thumbnail.png)](/docs/switches/step-1.png)

2. Place a switch at each corner of the top side of the switch plate.
    * Make sure they clip securely into the switch plate.

    [![TODO](/docs/switches/step-2_thumbnail.png)](/docs/switches/step-2.png)
    [![TODO](/docs/switches/step-2-2_thumbnail.png)](/docs/switches/step-2-2.png)

3. Position the switch plate onto the PCB.
    * Align the switches on the corners with their corresponding hot-swap socket.
4. Press the switches into their socket.
    * The switches should now sit flush with the PCB.

    [![TODO](/docs/switches/step-4_thumbnail.png)](/docs/switches/step-4.png)

5. Install the remaining switches.
    1. Position the switch onto the switch plate.
        * Be careful when positioning the thumb cluster switches.
        * Be careful when positioning the switches near the battery; make sure the battery cable is out of the way.
    2. Press the switch into the switch plate and the hot-swap socket.
    3. Repeat the previous steps for the remaining 30 switches.
