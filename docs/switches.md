# 13. Switches

> **Before starting:** Check that all switch pins are straight. Bent pins will not seat properly into the hotswap sockets and can damage them.

1. Place a switch at each of the **four corners** of the switch plate. Make sure they clip securely into the plate.
   ![Switch plate 4 corners](/docs/switches-and-switch-plate/switch-plate-4-corners.png)
2. Position the switch plate over the PCB, aligning the corner switches with their hotswap sockets.
3. Press the corner switches into the sockets to hold the plate in place.
   ![Switch plate positioned](/docs/switches-and-switch-plate/switch-plate-positioned.png)
4. Install the remaining switches one by one, pressing each into its socket while verifying it clips securely into the switch plate.
5. Before installing switches near the battery connector, make sure the battery cable is routed out of the way.
   ![Switch plate filled](/docs/switches-and-switch-plate/switch-plate-filled.png)

> **Tip:** Work from the corners inward to keep the plate aligned.

> **Verify:** No switches should wobble. All switches should sit flush against the PCB with no gaps. Each switch should be firmly clipped into the switch plate.
