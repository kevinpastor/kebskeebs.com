The 35 Kailh MX hotswap sockets go on the **back side** of the PCB, in the outlined footprints. The socket shape matches the footprint outline, so orientation is clear.

The following steps apply to **all 35 sockets**. Complete all steps for each socket one at a time, or perform each step across all sockets before moving to the next.

1. Place the socket into their footprints.
    ![Hotswap socket positioning](/docs/hotswap-sockets/hotswap-socket-positioning.png)
2. Solder one pad on each socket first, pressing the socket flat with a finger or tweezers as you go. Sockets can pop up from the heat if not held down.
    ![Hotswap socket soldered right pad](/docs/hotswap-sockets/hotswap-socket-soldered-right-pad.png)
3. Go back and solder the second pad on all sockets. The board can be rotated to make this easier.
    ![Hotswap sockets soldered overview](/docs/hotswap-sockets/hotswap-sockets-soldered-overview.png)
    ![Hotswap sockets soldered left side](/docs/hotswap-sockets/hotswap-sockets-soldered-left-side.png)

> **Verify:** Press each socket with a fingernail. It should not move or wiggle. Check that both pads have solid, shiny joints with no bridges between them.
