# 10. Case Back Plate

![Switch plate overview](/docs/switches-and-switch-plate/switch-plate-overview.png)

1. Insert the four **M2 x 7 mm standoffs** through the four mounting holes in the **PCB** from the bottom side.
2. Secure each standoff by threading an **M2 x 5 mm screw** through the corresponding hole in the **switch plate** from the top.
3. Place the back plate against the bottom of the standoffs.
4. Fasten the back plate to the standoffs using the remaining four **M2 x 5 mm screws**.

> **Tip:** Don't overtighten the screws. Snug is enough — overtightening can crack PCB-based plates.

> **Verify:** The back plate should sit flush against the standoffs with no gaps. The assembly should feel rigid with no flex.
