# 7. Microcontroller

![nice!nano overview](/docs/microcontroller/microcontroller-overview.png)

Solder the pin headers to the nice!nano so it can plug into the Mill-Max sockets.

> **Important:** Positioning is extremly important. Double check before soldering. The microcontroller must be **facing down**. The microcontroller **top hole**, labeled `B+` and `B-`, **must be left empty**.

1. Insert the two 12-pin pin headers (short side down) into the Mill-Max sockets on the PCB.
   ![Pin headers positioned](/docs/microcontroller/microcontroller-positioned-pin-header.png)
2. Position the microcontroller **face down** (components and logo facing down, away from you) with top socket empty onto the pin headers.
   ![Microcontroller positioned](/docs/microcontroller/microcontroller-positioned.png)
3. **Verify positioning**.
4. Check that the USB-C port sits at the edge of the PCB.
5. Solder the two corner pins first while keeping the microcontroller flat and flush.
   ![Microcontroller soldered extremities](/docs/microcontroller/microcontroller-soldered-extremities.png)
6. Verify alignement.
7. Solder the remaining pins.
   ![Microcontroller soldered](/docs/microcontroller/microcontroller-soldered.png)
8. Lift the microcontroller straight up to remove it from the sockets. Pull evenly from both sides to avoid bending pins.

   ![Microcontroller removal](/docs/microcontroller/microcontroller-removal.png)
   ![Microcontroller removed](/docs/microcontroller/microcontroller-removed.png)
